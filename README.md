## Featured projects

### 💰 [Sumant](https://sumant.app) · Personal finance, without the noise

A personal finance application built end-to-end with a **local-first architecture**. The app writes to IndexedDB first and synchronizes with Supabase in the background, with bank statement imports, budgets, goals, multiple accounts and on-device intelligent categorization.

`React` `TypeScript` `IndexedDB` `Dexie` `Supabase` `PWA` `Next.js`

[Live product →](https://sumant.app) · [Case study →](https://mroscar.dev/proyectos/sumant)

---

### 🤖 TodoPlagas AI · AI automation in production

A production system that automates customer support and lead handling through **WhatsApp and phone calls**, combining AI agents, persistent memory, asynchronous processing, CRM integrations, human handoff and error monitoring.

`n8n` `Python` `OpenAI` `PostgreSQL` `RabbitMQ`

[Case study →](https://mroscar.dev/proyectos/todoplagas)

---

### 🌊 [Subwave](https://subwave.pro) · AI subtitles entirely in your browser

Open-source subtitle editor that performs transcription, translation and video processing **fully client-side** — no backend and no media uploads. Built around local models, Web Workers, WebAssembly and WebCodecs.

`TypeScript` `Whisper` `Web Workers` `WebAssembly` `WebCodecs` `Astro`

[Live demo →](https://subwave.pro) · [Source →](https://github.com/mroscardev91/subwave)

---

### 🏥 [InvenMedPro](https://github.com/mroscardev91/InvenMedPro) · Medical inventory management

Full-stack application for inventory and operational management in medical centers, with authentication, roles, stock management and an administrative interface.

`Laravel` `PHP` `React` `Inertia.js` `MySQL`

[Source →](https://github.com/mroscardev91/InvenMedPro)
